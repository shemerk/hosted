Privacy Policy – Life Reset RPG

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Life Reset RPG ("the game") is developed by Shemer K ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the game collects, why, and how it is used. The game does not require an account or login.

1. Data We Collect

  • Anonymous identifier – On first launch the game creates an anonymous Google Firebase ID for your install. It is not linked to your name, Google account, or any other personal account.
  • Gameplay statistics – Progress and usage statistics (for example the hero name you choose, hero level, difficulty, shards reached, battles won and lost, achievements, time played, platform, app version, last active time) are sent to Google Firebase/Firestore under that anonymous ID. We use them to balance the game and understand where players get stuck.
  • Approximate location (country only) – Once per install, the game looks up your country from your IP address using the third-party service ipwho.is. Only the country is stored. We do not collect precise location, and we do not store your IP address.
  • Bug reports (voluntary) – If you send a bug report from the in-game Settings, we receive your message, the anonymous ID, app version, platform, hero level, current shard, country, and a copy of your local game save (to reproduce the problem). You may optionally add an email address so we can reply; it is used only to respond to that report.
  • Advertising – The game uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, and ad interaction data in accordance with Google's advertising policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Update and configuration checks – The game contacts our Firebase backend to check for updates and load game configuration. No personal data is sent for these checks.

Your game save itself is stored locally on your device. It is only sent to us if you attach it by submitting a bug report.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, account credentials, contacts, photos, or precise location.
  • We do not collect your email address unless you choose to include it in a bug report.
  • We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for their own marketing.

3. Third-Party Services

The game uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase / Firestore – anonymous ID, gameplay statistics, bug reports
  • Google AdMob – rewarded video ads
  • ipwho.is – one-time country lookup from IP address
  • Google Play Services

4. Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

5. Children's Privacy

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay statistics are kept while the game is in use and may be retained in aggregate afterwards. Bug reports are retained for up to 12 months. Uninstalling the game stops all further collection. To request deletion of data associated with your install or a bug report you sent, email us (include the approximate date of the report, or the email address you used in it, so we can find it).

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
