Privacy Policy – Life Reset RPG

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Life Reset RPG ("the game") is developed by Shemer K ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how it is used.

1. Data We Collect

  • Game save data – Your progress, hero stats, and settings are stored locally on your device and optionally synced to Google Firebase/Firestore to enable cloud saving.
  • Bug reports – If you submit a bug report via the in-game tool, the report text and basic device info are sent to Google Firestore. This is voluntary.
  • Advertising identifiers – We use Google AdMob to serve rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect your advertising ID and usage data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • App version checks – The game contacts our Firebase backend on launch to check for required updates. No personal data is transmitted.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, or any account credentials.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • We do not collect precise location data.

3. Third-Party Services

The game uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase / Firestore – cloud save and crash/bug reporting
  • Google AdMob – rewarded video ads
  • Google Play Services

4. Children's Privacy

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

5. Data Retention

Cloud save data is retained as long as you use the game. You may request deletion by contacting us. Bug reports are retained for up to 12 months.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
